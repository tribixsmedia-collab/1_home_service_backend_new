This policy explains what {{ company }} collects when you use our customer app, our vendor app or our website, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do about it. It covers both apps, because they share one system.

What we collect

If you book services as a customer

  • Your phone number. This is how you sign in — we send a six-digit code to it. It is also how a vendor reaches you about a job.
  • Your name, and your email address if you choose to sign in by email instead.
  • Your address — street address, state, district and PIN code — so a vendor knows where to come.
  • Your location, if you allow it. Used to show services available near you and to fill in your address from a map pin. You can refuse this and type your address instead; the app still works.
  • What you book and view — your bookings, their status, and the services you have looked at.
  • Anything you upload — photographs, drawings or plans attached to a project request, and messages you send to support.

If you work through us as a vendor

Everything above, and because we are paying you and putting you in front of customers:

  • Identity and business documents — government ID proof, address proof and trade certificates, so we can verify you are who you say you are before sending you to somebody's home.
  • Bank details — account holder name, account number, IFSC code, and UPI ID if you give one, so your money can reach you.
  • Where you work — the states and districts you cover.
  • Job photographs you take to record work in progress.

What we never collect

We do not see or store your card number, UPI PIN, CVV, or bank password. When you pay, the payment screen belongs to Razorpay, not to us. We are told only whether a payment succeeded and a reference number for it.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your information, and we do not share it for advertising. We use the following companies to run the service. Each receives only what it needs to do its job:

CompanyWhat it handles
RazorpayPayments, and transfers to vendors
CloudinaryStoring and delivering photographs and video
Google (Firebase)Sending notifications to your phone
Our SMS providerDelivering your sign-in code
Amazon Web ServicesRunning our servers and sending our email
Our map providerShowing maps and turning a pin into an address

A vendor assigned to your booking is shown your name, phone number and address, because they cannot do the work otherwise. They are not given your email or your payment details.

We may also disclose information where the law requires it — a court order, a tax authority, or a lawful request from the police.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Our servers are in India. Photographs and video are held by Cloudinary, which may store them outside India.

We keep your account and booking history while your account is open. If you close your account we delete your personal details, but we keep the financial record of completed bookings — the amount, the date and the invoice — because tax law requires us to. Vendor identity documents are deleted once they are no longer needed for verification.

How it is protected

The connection between the apps and our servers is encrypted. Vendor identity documents are deliberately not stored on a public image service: they sit on our own server and can only be opened by an administrator who has been granted access to them, one document at a time.

No system is perfect. If we discover a breach affecting your personal information, we will tell you.

What you can ask us to do

  • See what we hold about you. Ask, and we will tell you.
  • Correct it. Most of it you can edit yourself in the app.
  • Delete it. See Delete My Data for how, and what we are obliged to keep.
  • Withdraw permission for location or notifications, in your phone's settings. The app keeps working without them.

Children

This service is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has given us information, write to us and we will remove it.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
