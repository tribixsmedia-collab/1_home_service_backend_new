You can ask us to delete your account and the personal information attached to it. This page explains how, what goes, and what we are required to keep.

How to ask

  • the phone number you sign in with
  • whether the account is a customer or vendor account
  • the words “delete my account”

We will confirm we have received your request, check that it really is your account, and complete the deletion within 30 days.

You do not need the app installed to make this request, and there is no charge.

What gets deleted

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • Your address and any saved location
  • Your profile and sign-in credentials
  • Photographs and files you uploaded
  • Your notification tokens, so we stop contacting your phone
  • Your support messages
  • Vendors: your identity documents and your bank details

What we have to keep, and why

We cannot delete everything, and it would be dishonest to imply otherwise.

  • Records of completed bookings and payments — the amount, the date and the invoice. Indian tax and accounting law requires a business to retain these, normally for eight years. They are kept separately from your profile, and the personal details attached to them are reduced to what the law requires.
  • Anything subject to an active dispute — a refund under investigation, a complaint, or a legal claim — until it is resolved.

Once those periods pass, the remaining records are deleted too.

If you are a vendor with money owed

Deleting your account does not cancel a payment we owe you. Tell us before you request deletion so any pending payout can be completed first — once the bank details are gone we cannot pay you without asking for them again.

Deleting is permanent

Once done, your booking history, saved addresses and reviews are gone and cannot be restored. Signing up again later creates a new, empty account.
